⚑ Bio
THE FOOL is a Calgary-based producer, DJ, and professional narcissist. A self-described budding supervillain and child of DOOM, THE FOOL crafts dark, hard-hitting electronic music that blends elements of trap, phonk, and bass music with an unhinged, chaotic energy.

Drawing from a deep well of influence from the underground, THE FOOL's sound is aggressive, cinematic, and built for maximum destruction on any sound system. Each track is a statement — a middle finger to convention and a love letter to the loud, the reckless, and the foolish.
